3-Phase Motor Data

FLC, Starter & Protection

Pick a voltage and horsepower to pull NEC full-load current, NEMA starter size, minimum conductor, and branch-circuit protection sizing.

Full-load current from NEC Table 430.250 (3-phase, other than wound-rotor). NEMA starter size from NEMA ICS 2 full-voltage, non-plugging ratings — 460V and 575V share the same size chart. Minimum copper conductor is 125% × FLC (NEC 430.22), checked against Table 310.16's 60°C column when the branch-circuit breaker is 100A or less and the 75°C column above that, per 110.14(C) — the size shown applies to THW, THHN-THWN, and XHHW alike, since equipment terminations (not the conductor's own insulation rating) set the applicable column, and this assumes equipment that isn't marked for a higher rating. Branch-circuit protection uses NEC Table 430.52 percentages for other than wound-rotor/synchronous motors — 175% dual-element fuse, 250% inverse-time breaker, 800% instantaneous-trip breaker — rounded up to the next standard device rating (240.6(A)); these are the code-maximum ratings, and installed/combination-starter equipment is often protected at a smaller size than this ceiling. Field estimate only; verify against the NEC edition adopted by your AHJ and the motor's actual nameplate.

Transformer FLC

Single & Three-Phase FLC

Enter a transformer's kVA rating to get full-load current at common secondary voltages.

kVA

Single-phase FLC = kVA × 1000 ⁄ V. Three-phase FLC = kVA × 1000 ⁄ (√3 × V). This is the transformer's rated full-load current at each voltage, not a substitute for its nameplate current, and doesn't include overcurrent protection sizing (NEC Table 450.3) or inrush.

Per NEC 314.16(B): each conductor counts once at its own size (18–6 AWG only — larger conductors fall under 314.28, covered in the Pull / Junction Box mode above). Clamps count one allowance total (any number present) at the largest conductor size; each fixture stud/hickey and each device (2× per device) count at the largest conductor size; grounding conductors count one allowance total at the largest conductor size, plus one more if an isolated ground is also present. Box volumes are from Table 314.16(A) for standard metal boxes — always use the volume marked on the box itself when present.

For conductors 4 AWG and larger: size a pull or junction box per NEC 314.28(A).

Straight pulls: minimum box length ≥ 8× the trade diameter of the largest raceway entering the box. Angle or U pulls: minimum distance from that wall's raceway entries to the opposite wall ≥ 6× the trade diameter of the largest raceway on that wall, plus the sum of the trade diameters of every other raceway entering on the same wall. Applies to conductors 4 AWG and larger (insulated) or their cables, including splices. Covers a single wall/row only — consult NEC 314.28(A) directly for multiple entry rows or mixed conductor sizes.
